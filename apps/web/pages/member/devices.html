How connection will work

  1. Install the approved EA or companion on your Windows PC or VPS.
  2. Request a short-lived pairing code while signed in.
  3. Your device connects outbound over HTTPS.
  4. Confirm the broker, server and account you observed.
  5. Grant read-only access and revoke it whenever needed.
