Import MT5 statement

MT5 desktop: History → choose the period → Report → HTML. Upload the original English statement, up to 2 MB, with its Deals table.

Check your broker's server clock. This offset applies to every trade in this file. If the broker changed its clock, export separate periods with the correct offset. Imported dates are converted to MYT.

Nothing will be saved until you review and confirm.

Review your import

Only your own account can be imported. Existing executions are deduplicated; conflicting records are rejected. This is an uploaded statement, not proof of broker performance.
