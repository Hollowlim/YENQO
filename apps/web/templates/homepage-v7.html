04 · Security

Your account stays yours.

What YENQO can and cannot do with your trading.

Your money stays at your broker

YENQO cannot deposit, withdraw or move money in your broker account.

Credentials stay on the server

Your MT5 details are kept on our server, never in your browser.

Only you see your account

Each member sees only their own account and results.

Admins cannot trade for you

Administrators and mentors never get trading access to your broker account.

Trading is switched off

Broker execution is disabled. Automation will only start after separate testing.

Demo is always labelled

Charts and numbers say when they are a demonstration and not live data.
